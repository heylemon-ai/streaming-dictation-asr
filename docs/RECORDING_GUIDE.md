# Recording guide

## Before recording
- The speaker has signed the consent form.
- Assign a speaker id (e.g. `spk07`) and record it in every row of `transcripts.tsv`. Never use names.

## Equipment and environment
Record a mix, and note each in the clip metadata:
- Devices: built-in laptop mic, wired headset, wireless earbuds
- Distance: normal (30–50 cm), close, far (1 m+)
- Environment: quiet room, fan or air conditioning, café or street noise

## What to read or say
- Scripts written for real apps: chat messages, emails, notes, prompts for AI tools
- Include names, numbers, dates, times, product names, email and web addresses
- Include self-corrections ("Thursday, no wait, Friday") and spoken punctuation ("comma", "new line")
- Some natural, unscripted dictation, transcribed afterwards
- Some whispered takes and some with long pauses mid-sentence

## Format
- 16 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV
- No trimming or processing after recording
- File names: `<number>-<app>-<condition>.wav`
